Marriage Biodata Format for Boy

A marriage biodata for a boy is a short profile that introduces him and provides important information to a prospective bride and her family. A well-organized biodata can include personal details, education, profession, family information, lifestyle details and contact information.

Tip: Keep the biodata clear, honest and easy to read. Include the details that are relevant for a marriage proposal and avoid unnecessary personal information.

What to Include in a Boy's Marriage Biodata

The exact format can be customized according to personal and family preferences. A commonly used structure includes the following sections.

Section Details
Personal Details Name, date of birth, time of birth, place of birth, height, religion, caste/community, gotra, rashi and nakshatra.
Education Highest qualification, degree, university or other relevant educational information.
Career Profession, job title, company or business and other career-related information.
Lifestyle Languages, hobbies, interests and other relevant lifestyle information.
Family Details Father's name and occupation, mother's name and occupation, siblings and family background.
Contact Details Contact person, phone number, email, city and residential location as appropriate.

Boy Marriage Biodata Checklist

Before sharing your biodata, check that the important information is complete and presented consistently.

  • Use a clear and recent profile photograph if you choose to include one.
  • Check your name, date of birth and other personal details for accuracy.
  • Mention your education and current profession clearly.
  • Include relevant family information.
  • Keep contact information accurate and appropriate for sharing.
  • Use a clean layout that is easy to read on both mobile phones and computers.
