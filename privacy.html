PRIVACY POLICY

Your information matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how VivahBio handles information submitted through the website.

How information is used

Information is used to create, save, verify and provide biodata and downloads, process premium access, provide support, prevent misuse and maintain the service.

Photos

Uploaded photographs may be stored in configured storage so they can be used to generate the selected biodata.

Data deletion

To request removal of your profile information or photograph, contact rahulk.mgr08@gmail.com with sufficient information to identify the record. Requests are reviewed subject to operational and legal requirements.

Changes
