What Is a Marriage Biodata?

A marriage biodata is a short profile prepared for matrimonial introductions. It usually presents personal information, education, profession, family details, interests and contact information in an easy-to-read format.

A good biodata should be clear, accurate and neatly arranged. Include only the details you are comfortable sharing and that are relevant to your matrimonial introduction.

Simple Marriage Biodata Format

Name: Your Full Name

Date of Birth: DD/MM/YYYY

Time of Birth: Optional

Place of Birth: City, State

Height: Optional

Religion / Caste / Gotra: Optional

Rashi / Nakshatra: Optional

Education: Highest qualification

Profession: Job / Business / Occupation

Company: Optional

Languages: Languages known

Hobbies: Interests and hobbies

Father: Name and occupation

Mother: Name and occupation

Siblings: Brother / sister details

About Me: A short personal introduction

Contact: Contact person, city, phone or email

Details to Include in a Marriage Biodata

1. Personal Details

Name, date of birth, place of birth and other relevant personal details.

2. Education & Profession

Education, occupation, company or business details and professional information.

3. Family Details

Parents' names and occupations, siblings and other family information you choose to share.

4. About Me & Hobbies

A short introduction about personality, interests, hobbies and lifestyle.

5. Photo

A clear, recent and appropriate photograph can be added to the biodata.

6. Contact Details

Add a suitable contact person and contact information for matrimonial communication.

Marriage Biodata Format for Boy

A boy's marriage biodata can follow the same basic structure: personal details, education, profession, family information, hobbies and contact details. Depending on preference, details such as height, religion, caste, gotra, rashi and nakshatra can also be included.

Keep professional information concise and use a short “About Me” section to give a genuine introduction.

Marriage Biodata Format for Girl

A girl's marriage biodata can also use the same clear structure. Include personal, educational, professional and family details that are relevant to the matrimonial introduction.

Optional fields such as height, religion, caste, gotra, rashi, nakshatra, languages and hobbies can be added according to personal preference.

Tips for a Good Marriage Biodata

  • Keep the information accurate and easy to read.
  • Use a clear and recent photograph if you choose to include one.
  • Avoid unnecessarily long paragraphs.
  • Highlight education and profession clearly.
  • Keep family information organized and concise.
  • Review phone numbers and other contact details before sharing.
  • Choose a design that looks clean and professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should be included in a marriage biodata?

Common sections include personal details, education, profession, family details, hobbies, a short introduction, photograph and contact information. Optional matrimonial details can be added based on personal preference.

There is no single mandatory format. A good format is one that presents relevant information clearly and is comfortable for the person sharing it.
